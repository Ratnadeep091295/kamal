Every pound matters. Our lightweight equipment is selected for payload efficiency, controlled discharge and dependable performance.

The moving floor carries material through the rear while the trailer body stays down. This supports horizontal unloading at suitable receiving sites, including locations with limited overhead clearance.

Safety by Design

Our Deloupe walking floors unload with the trailer body down, helping reduce tipping risks.

Matching the floor to the material

Material density, abrasion, moisture and the receiving site all affect equipment selection. Different moving-floor profiles suit different materials, so we review the material and discharge requirements before planning the move.

A walking floor unloads horizontally. Tipper equipment relies on a suitable tipping arrangement at the destination. The right approach depends on both the material and the facility.
